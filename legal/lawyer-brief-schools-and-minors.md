# Brief for a lawyer — Planora: school use and minors

RESEARCH NOTES — NOT LEGAL ADVICE — prepared by app-legal-guard on 2026-10-10 to make a consultation short.

**Who:** Jonathan, student, building the app as a natural person in the Netherlands (no company, no KvK registration).
**The app:** Planora is a free planning app for students and teachers (website/PWA, desktop, Android). All user data stays on the user's device; there is no server and no account. Optional connections to Microsoft 365 (calendar, To Do, Teams assignments, OneDrive app folder for end-to-end encrypted sync) and Google Calendar free/busy run directly between the device and Microsoft/Google. Users: likely include secondary-school pupils (under 16). Revenue: none now; maybe later.

**The questions:**
1. If a school (or teachers) recommend or roll out Planora to pupils, what is my role under the GDPR — am I a processor for the school, or not processing at all because data never reaches me? Do I need a processing agreement (e.g. the education sector's model agreement / Privacyconvenant) to be allowed in schools?
2. Is the age wording in my privacy policy and terms ("under 16: ask a parent before connecting a personal Microsoft/Google account") adequate, given Art. 8 GDPR / UAVG art. 5 and that Planora itself collects nothing?
3. As a natural person without a company, how exposed am I personally if something goes wrong (e.g. a bug deletes a pupil's planning before a test)? Do my terms' liability limits hold against consumers/minors? Should I set up a company before wider distribution?

**Facts:** see `legal/app-inventory.md` (with code evidence) and `legal/dpia-screen.md`.
**What I already did:** privacy policy and terms in Dutch and English (drafts), least-privilege scopes, local-only storage with encryption, no tracking, delete-everything, public-report warning.
**Options I see:** (a) keep Planora as a personal tool pupils install themselves, no school rollout; (b) offer schools a standard information sheet + processing agreement; (c) form a company before school deployment.
